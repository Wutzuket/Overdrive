# Overdrive Wiki

Welcome to the **Overdrive** documentation.

Overdrive is a technology mod for Minecraft 1.21.1 and NeoForge. It adds
large-scale energy production, antimatter processing, cooling systems,
multiblock machines, fluids, scanners and programmable holograms.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.21.1
- NeoForge 21.1.x
- Java 21
- JEI is recommended for viewing recipes

The One Probe and Connected Textures Mod are optional integrations.

## Finding recipes

Overdrive integrates with JEI. Use JEI to inspect machine recipes, processing
chains and the resources required by each system. The Overdrive scanner can
also identify supported multiblock components and expose scanner recipes.

## Support

When reporting a problem, include the Minecraft and NeoForge versions, the
Overdrive version, the relevant log or crash report, and the steps required to
reproduce the issue.
